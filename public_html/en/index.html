What state
is the channel
of your business in?

Before proposing anything, we identify which of these three states your business is in. The answer determines where to start. A river that doesn't flow isn't fixed by pouring more water into it: it's fixed by clearing what's in the way.

State 01

Dry river

The business falls short. What comes in gets lost before it reaches the end. The channel is too wide for the flow it carries, and it evaporates along the way.

  • Customer information lost between WhatsApp, paper and the owner's head
  • Repetitive manual tasks that eat up the day without results
  • Operations too scattered for the real size of the business
  • Business data stored but nobody looking at it

State 02

Overflowing river

The business grew but the processes didn't. The channel can't hold the flow and spills everywhere. You spend all day putting out fires.

  • Orders lost between WhatsApp, emails, notebooks and people's heads
  • Employees whose work only the owner understands
  • Invoicing, inventory and customers in scattered files
  • The business can't run for 3 days without you

State 03

River with rocks

The business works, but there are clear rocks along the way. You've invested in technology before and something was left half done. You suspect you're overspending without noticing.

  • Software you pay for that nobody uses, or uses halfway
  • Manual processes that could run on their own
  • Decisions made on gut feeling because there's no visibility
  • Specific bottlenecks that repeat every month

How we work

Four steps.
No shortcuts.
In strict order.

This is the order every project follows. We don't skip steps to speed up the sale. We don't implement anything before understanding what your business needs. If your river is dry, we won't sell you automations: first we help you stop losing the little that comes in.

01

Diagnosis

We talk with the owner and the team. We review what you already have. We map the critical processes. We identify the state of the channel and where the leaks or rocks are.

  • Conversation with the owner and the team
  • Review of the tools, data and processes you already have
  • Identification of weak points, leaks and things to improve

OutcomeDiagnostic report: weak points and improvement opportunities, prioritized in a roadmap by return on investment.

02

Data

We find out what information your business generates, where it's being lost and how to turn it into something useful for decisions. Without data there are no efficient processes or engineering worth doing.

  • Stakeholder map: who generates, uses and decides with each piece of data
  • Where information is lost, how good it is and what is worth measuring
  • Integration of what lives today in Siigo, Alegra, WhatsApp or spreadsheets

OutcomeStakeholder and information flow diagram: who generates each piece of data, where it travels and where it gets lost.

The foundation of everything

03

Processes

We redesign key processes so the river flows without waste. We automate only what should be automated. We clean up before we build.

  • Mapping of key processes exactly as they work today
  • Redesign removing unnecessary steps and bottlenecks
  • Automation only of what should be automated (n8n or custom scripting)

OutcomeBPMN diagrams of your processes: how they work today and how they should flow.

Efficiency first

04

Engineering

Only here do custom software, integrations, applied artificial intelligence or infrastructure come in. Only when the diagnosis justified it. Built with you, not over the wall.

  • Systems, network and infrastructure architecture before building
  • Custom software, integrations and applied artificial intelligence
  • Implementation and monitoring, side by side with your team

OutcomeArchitecture diagrams of your systems, network and infrastructure, and the solution running with your team using it.

Frequently asked questions

What you usually
ask before
writing to us.

How are you different from an agency or a traditional consulting firm?

An agency sells you pre-built packages (web, marketing, social media) without first understanding your business from the inside. A big consulting firm hands you a PowerPoint report and leaves. Cauce does both differently: an honest engineering diagnosis + hands-on implementation with the same people who did the diagnosis. You aren't handed off to some junior after the sale. There's no surprise "phase 2".

Why do you charge for a coffee when others offer free calls?

Because a free call is a sales pitch in disguise. Charging a small amount commits us to bringing you something of real value (actionable recommendations, not a sales presentation) and filters out people who only want to compare prices. In return you leave with concrete ideas you can apply that same day, whether you hire us or not.

What if after the coffee I decide not to work with you?

Perfect, that's your right. The coffee already did its job: you got real recommendations and we got the conversation. No penalties, no pressure. If you decide to move forward with a full diagnosis, the cost of the coffee is deducted from the proposal.

Do you only work with companies in Valle del Cauca?

We're based in Cali and know the local context well, but we work with clients throughout Colombia and Latin America, and remotely in any country. Café Cauce can be in person (if you're in Valle del Cauca) or by video call. For large projects we can make occasional visits to any city.

What kind of technology do you implement?

Whatever your business needs, not whatever is trendy. For automations we use tools like n8n or custom scripting. For custom systems we use modern, stable technologies. We integrate with what you already use (Siigo, Alegra, WhatsApp Business, Google Workspace, World Office). If you need something more specific, let's talk.

Do you build websites, do marketing or manage social media?

Websites and online stores, yes: we design them, connect them to your orders, inventory and WhatsApp, and manage them as webmasters. Marketing, advertising and social media management, no; for that we recommend good agencies in Valle del Cauca that specialize in it.

Do you have formal contracts and invoicing?

Yes. Cauce operates as a registered Colombian company with a tax ID (NIT), electronic invoicing and a service agreement. Everything is formal so your accounting can record it without issues.
