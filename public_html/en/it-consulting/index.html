Consulting

Consulting

Our four-stage method applied to your business: Diagnosis → Data → Processes → Engineering. It is the base of the tower: it decides which pieces you need and in what order.

See what it includes
  • Diagnosis: we talk with the owner and the team, review what you already have and find where time or money is being lost.
  • Data: what information your business generates, where it gets lost and what is worth measuring.
  • Processes: what to redesign before buying or building technology.
  • Engineering: a roadmap ordered by return on investment, with the pieces that come next.

Ideal if You don't know where to start, or you've already invested in technology and it didn't pay off.

Architecture

Network and software architecture

The design of your network infrastructure or your software systems, before buying equipment or writing a line of code.

See what it includes
  • Software: we define the components, data and integrations so that whatever gets built, with or without AI, can grow.
  • Networks: topology, segmentation and cabling versus Wi-Fi for a new location or a network that can no longer cope.
  • Servers: cloud, on-premise or hybrid: what's right for your business, not the most expensive or the trendiest.
  • Blueprints and documentation so that we or any provider can build on them.

Ideal if You have a software idea, you're opening a new location, or your network and systems can't keep up.

Security and pentest

Security audit and pentesting

We audit your software's security and find its weak points before an attacker does, using the OWASP ASVS standard as reference.

See what it includes
  • Security audit: we verify your application against OWASP ASVS (Application Security Verification Standard), the standard for assessing application security.
  • Pentest: controlled penetration testing of your proprietary software, attacking it the way a real attacker would.
  • Review of access, passwords, permissions and exposed data.
  • Vulnerability report prioritized by risk, with how to fix each one.

Ideal if Your software handles customer data, payments or sensitive information, or you're about to launch a major version.

See audit and pentest →

Frequently asked questions

What people usually
ask us.

Do I have to hire all the pieces?

No. Each piece is hired separately and fits with the others. You can start with just one (for example, IT support) and add others when you need them.

I don't know which pieces I need. Where do I start?

With consulting or a Café Cauce. In one hour we review your case and tell you which pieces make sense and which don't.

Do you build websites, automations and AI agents?

Yes, within the custom development piece: corporate websites and their management, process automation with RPA, n8n or scripting, and artificial intelligence agents connected to your systems. As always, we understand the process first: we only automate what is worth it.

Is support on-site or remote?

Both. In Cali we come to your office when needed; elsewhere we help through a remote help desk with secure access. Consulting, development and security are delivered remotely to companies in Colombia, Latin America and any country.

How is it priced?

Each piece has a fixed scope and price, which we send in the proposal after understanding your case. No surprises and no unexpected "phase 2".
