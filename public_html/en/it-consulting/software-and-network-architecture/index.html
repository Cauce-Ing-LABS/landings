What we do

Three blueprints,
one single vision.

We define what your solution should look like before spending on equipment or programming hours.

01

Software architecture

We define the components, data and integrations of your system so it can be built in stages and grow.

  • Modules, database and integrations
  • What to do with AI and what not to
  • Well-justified technology decisions
02

Network design

Topology, segmentation and the choice between cabling and Wi-Fi for a new location or a network that can no longer cope.

  • Separate networks for checkout, office and guests
  • Placement of equipment and network points
  • Growth to new locations
03

Servers and cloud

Cloud, on-premise server or hybrid: what's right for your business, not the most expensive or the trendiest.

  • AWS, Google Cloud or Azure
  • Local server with backups
  • Estimated monthly costs
04

Integrations

How your systems connect: invoicing, inventory, online store, WhatsApp and spreadsheets.

  • Siigo, Alegra, WhatsApp Business
  • Which systems have an API and which don't
05

Security by design

Access, permissions and backups planned from the blueprint, not patched afterwards.

  • Who can see what
  • Backup and recovery plan
06

Documentation

Blueprints and documentation so that we or any provider can build on them.

  • Architecture diagrams
  • Network diagrams
  • Decisions and the reasons behind them

Frequently asked questions

What people usually
ask us.

Why pay for architecture if I can start programming?

Because fixing the design later costs much more. With a good blueprint, development is faster, cheaper and can grow without being rebuilt.

Does the architecture force me to develop with you?

No. The blueprints are in your name and any provider can build on them.

Do you also design the network for a store or a small office?

Yes. A store with a checkout, card terminal, cameras and customer Wi-Fi also needs a well-separated, secure network.
