What we do

Cauce's four stages,
in your business.

Diagnosis, Data, Processes and Engineering: our four-stage method applied to your business. It is the foundation for everything else: it decides which pieces you need and which you don't.

01

Diagnosis

We talk with the owner and the team and review what you already have: tools, software you pay for, spreadsheets and the real way you work.

  • Weak points, leaks of time and money
  • Software you pay for and don't use
  • Obvious security risks
02

Data

We find out what information your business generates, where it gets lost and what's worth measuring so you decide with numbers, not gut feeling.

  • Map of who generates, uses and decides with each piece of data
  • Information scattered across WhatsApp, paper and files
  • Integration with Siigo, Alegra or spreadsheets
03

Processes

We map key processes exactly as they work today and redesign them by removing unnecessary steps, before automating anything.

  • BPMN diagrams of how it works today and how it should
  • Bottlenecks that repeat every month
  • What to automate and what not to
04

Engineering

With all of the above we build the roadmap: what to build, buy or integrate, ordered by return on investment.

  • Recommended pieces and in what order
  • What you can do on your own and what needs help
  • Fixed scope and price for each step

Frequently asked questions

What people usually
ask us.

How long does a diagnosis take?

It depends on the size of the business and how many processes we review. The proposal gives you a fixed timeline and price before we start.

Do I have to hire you for the implementation?

No. The roadmap is yours: you can carry it out with your team, with another provider or with us.

Does it work for a small business?

Yes. We work with stores, neighborhood businesses and entrepreneurs, not just large companies. The diagnosis adapts to the size of your operation.
