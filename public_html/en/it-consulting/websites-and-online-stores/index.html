Frequently asked questions

What people usually
ask us.

Do you build online stores?

Yes. Catalog, cart and orders, with online payment or ordering via WhatsApp, and connected to your inventory when needed.

What is the webmaster service?

It's the management of your site: content changes, updates, backups, domain, hosting and security certificate. You focus on the business.

Do you manage social media or advertising?

No. We focus on the technical side: the site, the store, their security and their connection to your operations.

Can I update the products myself?

Yes. We leave the store ready for you or your team to update products, photos and descriptions without depending on us.
