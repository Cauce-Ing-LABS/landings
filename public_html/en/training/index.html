The programs

Three programs,
three levels each.

Every program follows the same logic: level 1 levels the base, level 2 builds autonomy, and the closing session turns what was learned into a concrete plan for your business.

For teams starting from scratch with technology.

Digital Literacy

“Nobody on your team should be afraid of the mouse anymore.”

  • First Digital Steps (12h): From never touching a computer to using it without help.
  • Office Tools for Your Business (12h): Quotes, inventory and reports, done by your team.
  • Closing: from theory to your processes (4h): They identify on their own what to automate, or hire you to do it.
To protect your business from the most common digital risks.

Cybersecurity

“One wrong click costs more than this whole program.”

  • Essential Cybersecurity (12h): So nobody on your team falls for the next scam.
  • Secure Information Management (12h): You decide who sees what, and what to do if something is lost.
  • Closing: from theory to your defense (4h): A real diagnosis of how exposed your business is today.
So your team starts using artificial intelligence in their daily work.

AI Adoption

“From asking Google everything to having AI work for you.”

  • AI for Everyday Work (12h): From not knowing what a prompt is to saving hours every week.
  • AI in Your Work Routine (12h): AI stops being a one-off trick and becomes part of the workflow.
  • Closing: from theory to your autonomy (4h): Together they find which tasks can be automated.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually
ask us.

Where are the training sessions held?

We're in Cali. Level 1 of every program is always in person; level 2 and the closing session can be in person or online. For teams outside Cali, write to us and we'll quote it.

How many people can take part?

Up to 20 people per level. For larger groups we set up several cohorts or a special quote.

Do I have to take the full program?

No. You can choose individual levels or full programs. Build the path the way you need it and we'll send you the proposal.

What happens after the closing session?

Each program's closing session ends with a document of findings and recommendations. You decide whether to implement it internally or hire Cauce to do it.
